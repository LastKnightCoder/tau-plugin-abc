# ABC Sheet Music

Write music notation using ABC notation in Tau documents on desktop and mobile, and preview the rendered staff notation right away. Scores are saved with your document and adapt automatically to Tau's light and dark themes.

## Getting Started

1. Install and enable **ABC Sheet Music** from the Plugin Center, then fully quit and reopen Tau.
2. Open or create a document in edit mode, type `/` or open the insert menu (`+`), search for "sheet music" or `abc`, and insert the music notation block.
3. Click the source edit button on the block, enter your ABC notation, and click **Save**. Your rendered score is saved with the document.

Paste the example below into the source editor to try it out:

```abc
X:1
T:Etude
M:4/4
L:1/4
K:C
C D E F | G A B c |
```

## FAQ

**Can it play audio or export MIDI?** No. This plugin focuses on writing and displaying staff notation in documents, without audio playback or MIDI export.

**Does it work on mobile?** Yes. You can insert, edit, and preview scores on desktop, Android, and iOS.

**Will disabling the plugin delete my scores?** No. Your ABC notation stays saved in the document and displays normally again after you re-enable the plugin and restart Tau.

## Tips

- After installing, enabling, disabling, or updating the plugin, fully quit and reopen Tau for the change to take effect.
